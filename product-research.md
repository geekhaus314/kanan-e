## Important Notes

### ⚠️ Product Discrepancies & Clarifications

1. **Marlboro Red 100s (#1):** The current official product name is **"Marlboro Red Label 100's Box"** — the "Red Label" designation replaced "Red" on packaging. The original "Marlboro 100's Box" (Carton UPC: 028200136305) is the legacy product. Both are Philip Morris USA products. The data above uses the current active UPC: 028200136909. Sources: [CA AG Tobacco Directory](https://oag.ca.gov/tobacco/directory), [PA AG UPC List](https://www.attorneygeneral.gov/wp-content/uploads/List-of-UPC-Codes.pdf), [MA DOR Directory](https://www.mass.gov/doc/phillip-morris-usa/download).

2. **Camel Crush 100s (#3):** **Camel Crush is NOT available in 100s (100mm) format.** It is only manufactured in King (85mm) size. The product listed above is **"Camel Crush King Box"** (Carton UPC: 012300197403). Other Camel Crush variants include: Crush Menthol King (012300197403), Crush Menthol Silver King (012300194136), Crush Rich King (012300108751), Crush Smooth Menthol King (012300119818), and Crush Bold (012300199414). All are King size only. Sources: [PA AG UPC List](https://www.attorneygeneral.gov/wp-content/uploads/List-of-UPC-Codes.pdf), [MA DOR RJR Directory](https://www.mass.gov/doc/rjr-tobacco/download), [Tobacco Wiki](https://tobacco.fandom.com/wiki/Camel).

3. **Newport Menthol 100s (#4):** The task lists the manufacturer as "ITG Brands" — this is **incorrect**. Newport is manufactured by **R.J. Reynolds Tobacco Company** (a subsidiary of Reynolds American Inc. / British American Tobacco). ITG Brands manufactures Winston, Kool, Salem, and Maverick (brands divested during the 2015 Reynolds-Lorillard merger), but Newport remained with Reynolds. Source: [CA AG Tobacco Directory](https://oag.ca.gov/tobacco/directory), [PA AG UPC List](https://www.attorneygeneral.gov/wp-content/uploads/List-of-UPC-Codes.pdf).

4. **Swisher Sweets Rillo (#5):** "Rillo" is shorthand for "cigarillo." The official Swisher Sweets product line calls these **"Swisher Sweets Cigarillos."** The 50ct box format is "10 Packs of 5 Cigarillos (50 Total)." Swisher also has a separate "Swisher Rillo" product line (at Blue Sky Sales) sold in different wholesale formats (e.g., "2 Boxes of 15/2 PK"). The closest match to "50ct Box" is the standard Swisher Sweets Cigarillos in 10/5 format. Source: [Swisher Sweets Official](https://swishersweets.com/), [LM Cigars](https://lmcigars.com/product/swisher-sweets-cigarillos/), [Blue Sky Sales](https://www.blueskysales.com/wholesale-tobacco-products/brands/SWISHER-SWEETS/custitemfeatured_products_california/false?page=2).

5. **Santa Cruz Shredder 4pc 60mm (#16):** Santa Cruz Shredder does **not** manufacture a 60mm grinder. Standard sizes are: **Small** (1.6" / 40mm), **Medium** (2.1" / 53mm), **Large** (2.75" / 70mm), and **Jumbo** (larger). The closest match to 60mm would be the **Medium** (53mm / 2.1"). The product listed above is the Small 4-piece. Source: [Planet of the Vapes](https://www.planetofthevapes.com/products/santa-cruz-4-piece-grinder), [Santa Cruz Shredder Official](https://santacruzshredder.com/).

6. **Geek Bar / RAZ (#8, #9):** Geek Bar and RAZ are both brands under **Geek Vape** (Shenzhen, China). The RAZ TN9000 is sometimes listed as "Geek Vape RAZ TN9000." Both products contain 5% (50mg) nicotine salt. Source: [Geek Bar Official](https://www.geekbar.com/product/meloso-max.html), [Vapor Authority](https://www.vaporauthority.com/products/geek-vape-raz-tn9000-disposable-vape), [Mi-One Brands](https://mipod.com/blogs/mipodblog/geek-bar-pulse-versus-raz-tn9000).

7. **VELO Citrus (#12):** VELO is manufactured by **R.J. Reynolds Vapor Company** (a subsidiary of Reynolds American / BAT). The Northerner listing shows "Manufacturer: Modoral Brand Inc" — this is likely a distributor label. The Prilla listing correctly shows "Manufacturer: Reynolds American Inc." Older VELO cans had 15 pouches; newer packaging may have 20 pouches. Source: [Northerner](https://www.northerner.com/us/velo-4mg-citrus-slim-nicotine-pouches), [Prilla](https://prilla.com/us/velo-nicotine-pouches/velo-citrus-4), [VELO Official](https://www.velo.com/us).

8. **ZYN FDA Authorization:** ZYN Cool Mint 3mg, Cool Mint 6mg, Peppermint 3mg, Peppermint 6mg, Spearmint 3mg, Spearmint 6mg, Wintergreen 3mg, Wintergreen 6mg, Citrus 3mg, and Citrus 6mg received FDA Marketing Granted Orders on January 16, 2025. Source: [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/media/188307/download), [PMI Press Release](https://www.pmi.com/investor-relations/press-releases-and-events/press-releases-overview/press-release-details?newsId=28291), [FDA MGO Letter](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/static/searchtobacco/2-6-25/MGO_Ltr_SMUSA_PM593-PM612_Zyn_MM_DD_2024_Redacted.pdf).

9. **Wholesale Prices:** Cigarette wholesale prices vary significantly by state due to excise taxes. The prices listed are retail/duty-free reference prices — actual wholesale prices require a tobacco license and vary by distributor and state. Cigar, vape, and accessory prices shown are from authorized distributor/retail sites.
